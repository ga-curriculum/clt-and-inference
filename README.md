## Foreword
This is a single lesson meant to span two lesson slots. Topics contained are the all-powerful Central Limit Theorem, hypothesis testing, and confidence intervals.

## Lesson Plan
By the end of this lesson, students should be able to:
* Clearly explain the concept of "inference" as it pertains to population parameters and sample statistics
* Carry out hypothesis test of one and two samples
* Correctly interpret p-values and the conclusions of hypothesis tests
* Carry out ANOVA tests and interpret ANOVA tables
* Compute confidence intervals and correctly interpret them.
